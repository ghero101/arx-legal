Convergence — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · Developer: Arx Laboratories

This policy explains how the Convergence app (com.arxlaboratories.convergence) handles information. Convergence is an app for couples: each partner answers questions privately on their own phone, and the app shows where you agree once you have both answered. Your answers live on your two phones. We run a relay that passes encrypted messages between them, and we cannot read what they contain.

What stays on your phones

  • Your answers, predictions, check-ins and notes are stored on your device, encrypted with a key protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Pairing happens in person, by scanning a code on your partner's phone. The two phones agree on keys that never leave them.
  • Answers you keep private are used to compare with your partner's and are never shown to them.

What our relay sees

To move data between the two phones, each phone deposits messages at our relay and collects the other's. Every message is end-to-end encrypted on the phone before it is sent and padded to a fixed size, so the relay cannot read it or tell one kind of message from another. The relay does see:

  • a random identifier for your couple and one for each phone, and each phone's public key, used to route messages and check which phone is talking;
  • when a phone deposits or collects messages, and how many.

Messages are deleted from the relay once the other phone has collected them, and in any case after 30 days. The relay keeps no long-term copy and does not store IP addresses. Connections reach it through Cloudflare, which handles them under its own privacy policy.

Backups

If you turn on backup, the app saves an encrypted copy of your data to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only this app can open that folder, and the copy is encrypted with your recovery words before it leaves your phone, so neither Google nor we can read it. We never receive it. You can also save an encrypted copy as a file yourself. Ending the couple deletes the Drive copy.

Google sign-in

The app asks you to sign in with Google. The sign-in token is checked on your phone and then discarded. We do not receive or store your name, email address or Google account details.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to scan the pairing code. No images are stored or sent.
  • Notifications — reminders and partner activity. They never contain your answers.
  • Biometrics — to lock the app on your phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising in the app.
  • We hold no key that could decrypt your answers, and there is no way for us to give anyone access to them.

Deleting your data

There is no account with us to delete. To remove your data, open the app, tap “Something has happened and I need this to stop” at the bottom of the home screen, and choose to end the couple. This deletes the couple's data from your phone and ends the couple identity permanently. It also deletes the backup in your Google Drive. Anything still waiting at the relay expires within 30 days. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on your phone. Your partner's phone keeps its own copy, which only they can delete.

Children

Convergence is intended for adults (17+) and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
