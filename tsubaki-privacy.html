Tsubaki — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 August 2026 · Developer: Arx Laboratories

This policy explains how the Tsubaki app (com.arxlaboratories.tsubaki) handles information. Tsubaki is a comic/manga reader that connects to a server you run, or runs on its own. We do not operate a service that collects your data.

Information the app uses

  • Your server connection — if you connect Tsubaki to your own self-hosted server, your library, reading progress, and account live on your server, not with us.
  • Local data — settings, reading progress, and downloaded content are stored on your device.
  • Third-party sources — when you browse or read content from external sources, the app connects directly to those sites to fetch it. Those requests are subject to those sites' own policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising in the app.
  • We do not have servers that receive your reading data.

Data retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. Data on a server you host is under your control. Questions? Contact us below.

Children

Tsubaki is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
