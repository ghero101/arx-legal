TCGBox — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 August 2026 · Developer: Arx Laboratories

This policy explains what information the TCGBox app (com.arxlaboratories.tcgbox) accesses, why, and how it is handled. TCGBox is a trading-card collection tracker. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Information the app uses

  • Camera — used only when you scan a card. Images are processed on your device to read the card (on-device text recognition). Photos are not uploaded to us and are not stored after the scan.
  • Your collection data (cards, quantities, storage labels) is stored locally on your device.
  • Google account (optional) — if you choose to sign in, the app uses your Google account to back up and sync your collection to your own Google Drive. We do not store your collection on our servers, and we do not access other files in your Drive.
  • Advertising — the app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and usage data to provide and measure ads. See Google's Ads & privacy policy.
  • Purchases — any in-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Card data — to show card details and images, the app requests public data from Scryfall (scryfall.com). These requests do not include your personal information.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your photos, contacts, location, or messages.
  • We do not run our own servers that store your collection.

Data sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app's features: Google (Sign-In, Drive, AdMob, Play Billing) and Scryfall (public card data). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Collection data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Any backup you created lives in your own Google Drive and can be deleted there. To ask a question or request deletion of anything associated with your use of the app, contact us below.

Children

TCGBox is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data stays on your device or in your Google account. Network requests use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
